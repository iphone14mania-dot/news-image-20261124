A clean newspaper front page that sums up Tuesday 24 November 2026 in one 1024×1024 image: 12 stories, one hero lead, no clutter. The hero is the world backing the ICJ's genocide verdict on Myanmar — fourteen nations endorse the ruling that Myanmar's forces committed genocide against the Rohingya, and Britain sanctions the generals. The two leads below it: NATO vowing to defend space after reports Russia may launch a nuclear-armed satellite within 60 days, and Nvidia reinstating Jensen Huang as CEO, sending markets surging (Nasdaq +6.5%, S&P 500 +4.7%). Three more cards cover the capture of "El Chapo Isidro" with US drone help, the Russian strike on Zaporizhzhia and Ukraine's confirmed ship strikes off Libya, and a M5.6 quake in Nepal — plus six one-line briefs (China's death sentence for spying, Russia's warning to Estonia, Gaza strikes, the Solomon Islands pact, the SpaceX lawsuit, Pennsylvania measles). The date is the masthead, so the day it sums up is unmistakable at a glance. Every claim is attributed to its source; nothing is presented as fact without attribution. The image is drawn programmatically by `draw24.py` (Pillow, original vector art).

mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
